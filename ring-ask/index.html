Reading preferences

Position preview only; the glasses font may look different.

Preferences save automatically. Long words may wrap sooner to fit the glasses. Choose line scrolling or full pages. Position moves the whole block without changing its width or line wrapping. A wider block has less room to move; reduce width only if you want more movement. The word count is a maximum, not a guaranteed number of words that fit. Glasses emphasize technical keywords with capitals; inline bold is unavailable. The glasses do not expose pixel-smooth animation.

For the closest perceived distance, open the Even app’s Display Adjustment and choose Near. The plugin can position text but cannot change optical distance or font size.
